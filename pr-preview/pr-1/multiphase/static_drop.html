surfaceTension/staticDrop¶

A static drop: the parasitic currents of the Brackbill continuum surface force (CSF) with gradAlpha curvature, in NeoFOAM’s full two-phase solver incompressibleVoFNeoN. The case is NeoFOAM’s test/solver/incompressibleVoFNeoN/cases/stationaryDroplet; native interFoam on the same case is the reference.

The drop should stay at rest, so every velocity is a spurious current, and the pressure jump should equal σ/R = 35 Pa. The solver evaluates the metrics in-run as NeoFOAM postProcess tables at the field write times; metrics.py derives the rest when they are collected.

The full sweep takes about 1 min with -c 1.

Larger meshes¶

deltaT 2e-5 is 0.57 of the capillary limit sqrt(rho_avg dx³ / (2π σ)) at n80, so a finer mesh needs a smaller time step too: add n160: {block_mesh_dict_config: {blocks: [{cells: [160, 160, 1]}]}} and a dimension control_dict_config: {dt2e-5: {deltaT: 2.0e-5}, dt5e-6: {deltaT: 5.0e-6}}. A new dimension adds its own check groups; narrow them with where.

All 9 cases and columns: results.csv, per-group checks: checks.json.

  • interFoam parity at n40: Δp = 29.90 Pa against interFoam’s 29.9009, max|U| at the end time 0.199 m/s.

  • Curvature converges slowly: Δp misses σ/R by 18 %, 14.7 % and 9.6 % at R/dx = 5, 10 and 20 (gradAlpha curvature; interFoam has the same deficit).

  • Spurious current grows with resolution at fixed deltaT: max|U| over time 0.15, 0.26, 0.42 m/s (Ca up to 6.1e-3), a known CSF property.

  • Executors: CPU and GPU agree with Serial to 1e-10.
