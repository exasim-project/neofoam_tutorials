reconstruction/circle¶

Interface reconstruction of a circle on hexahedral meshes. NeoFOAM’s gradAlpha (Youngs) PLIC reconstruction is compared with TwoPhaseFlow’s reconstructInterface and with numufoam’s published errors (Scheufler & Roenby, arXiv 1801.05382, sec. 4.1.1).

There is no flow and no time stepping: each sample is reconstructed once and compared with the exact circle. The alpha field agrees with OpenFOAM-v2406’s setAlphaField to round-off.

24 cases (8 × 3 × 1); the smoke run (--smoke) has 2. The raw payloads are in benchmarks/reconstruction/circle/benchmark.yaml.

The full sweep takes about 1 min with -c 4.

Larger meshes¶

Add n2048: {block_mesh_dict_config: {blocks: [{cells: [2048, 1, 2048]}]}} to mesh. The TwoPhaseFlow table ends at n1024, so n2048 enters only the plots. Case data grows with n² (the sweep to n1024 needs about 1.5 GB).

Rows: the Serial executor. CPU and GPU are bitwise identical to it in these columns.

All 24 cases and columns: results.csv, changes against the previous snapshot: changes.json.

  • Centre converges at 2nd order (fitted 1.92 in centre L1, 1.68 in centre L∞), as numufoam’s gradAlpha.

  • Normal error is flat: L∞ stays at 8.4e-3 and L1 at 2.4e-3 from n64 on. That is the gradAlpha limit, in OpenFOAM as in NeoFOAM; plicRDF is not yet in NeoN.

  • Parity with TwoPhaseFlow: the normal norms and centre L∞ agree to 4e-8; centre L1 differs by up to 4.2e-6 at n1024 because the volTaylor plane search stops within isoFaceTol = 1e-8 of the target volume.

  • Executors: Serial, CPU and GPU give identical norms; at n1024 the solver takes 12 / 11 / 9.8 s.
